# E-Commerce-Data-Analysis
🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard
🎯 Project Title & Purpose
This interactive Power BI dashboard provides a clear, high-level overview of overall sales performance, profitability, and regional distribution for an e-commerce business. It is designed to help stakeholders track sales trends over time, compare category margins, and make data-driven decisions to boost revenue and profitability.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Power BI: Used for creating data models, DAX measures, custom visual layouts, and interactive report filtering.
Power Query: Utilized for data cleaning, ETL workflows, date formatting, and structure preparation.

🏢 Business Problem & Dashboard Overview
Managing sales across multiple categories and regions makes it difficult to quickly spot low-margin product lines or underperforming territories. This dashboard breaks down overall sales performance into actionable visual segments so management can optimize marketing spend, adjust pricing strategies, and leverage Q4 holiday seasonality.

💡 Key Insights
Focus Marketing on High-Margin Categories: Shift digital ad spend toward Beauty (53.03% margin) and Clothing (42.78% margin). These two categories generate 52.9% of total net profits while making up only 35.2% of overall revenue.

Restructure Electronics Pricing: Re-evaluate discount strategies for Electronics. Despite generating the highest gross revenue ($28K), it yields the lowest profit margin (16.57%). Reducing heavy discounts on peripherals will protect profit margins.

Capitalize on Q4 Seasonality: Revenue consistently surges during the Q4 holiday period (November–December peak of ~$5.7K–$5.9K) compared to mid-year averages (~$3,000/month). Allocate stock inventory 6–8 weeks in advance.

Target Underperforming Regions: Tailor regional promotions for the West region, which trails all territories in revenue ($15.19K) despite maintaining a solid 31.18% profit margin.
