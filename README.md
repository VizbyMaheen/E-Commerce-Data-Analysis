# E-Commerce-Data-Analysis
🛒 Project Title & Purpose
This interactive Power BI dashboard provides a clear, high-level overview of overall sales performance, profitability, and regional distribution for an e-commerce business. It is designed to help stakeholders track sales trends over time, compare category margins, and make data-driven decisions to boost revenue and profitability.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Power BI: Used for creating data models, DAX measures, custom visual layouts, and interactive report filtering.
Power Query: Utilized for data cleaning, ETL workflows, date formatting, and structure preparation.

🏢 Business Problem & Dashboard Overview
Managing sales across multiple categories and regions makes it difficult to quickly spot low-margin product lines or underperforming territories. This dashboard breaks down overall sales performance into actionable visual segments so management can optimize marketing spend, adjust pricing strategies, and leverage Q4 holiday seasonality.

💡 Key Insights
Shift Promotion Focus to High-Margin Categories: Reallocate digital marketing spend toward Beauty & Clothing. These two categories currently account for 52.9% of total net profits while comprising only 35.2% of overall revenue.

Re-evaluate Pricing & Discount Strategies for Electronics: Restructure discounting on Electronics, which yields a low 16.57% profit margin despite bringing in highest revenue (30.7% of total revenue). Cease discounts on hardware peripherals (e.g., Portable Chargers, USB-C Hubs, Wireless Mice) to prevent further margin erosion.

Capitalize on Q4 Seasonality: Allocate budget and stock inventory 6–8 weeks ahead of the Q4 holiday spike (November–December), where revenue consistently peaks  compared to the mid-year average of $3,000/month.

Target Underperforming Regions for Growth: Tailor region-specific promotions in the West region, which trails all other territories in revenue ($15.19K) despite maintaining a healthy 31.18% profit margin.
